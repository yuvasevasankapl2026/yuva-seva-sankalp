About Us

Who We Are

Yuva Seva Sankalp Foundation is a non-profit organisation dedicated to helping poor families through education, healthcare, social welfare, employment support and community development.

Our Vision

To build a society where every individual gets equal opportunities for education, health, dignity and sustainable development.

Our Values

Transparency, Humanity, Equality, Service, Innovation, Responsibility and Community Participation.

Mission & Vision

Education

Scholarships, free coaching, digital literacy and quality education for every child.

Health

Health awareness camps, blood donation, free medical support and nutrition programmes.

Women Empowerment

Skill development, financial awareness and self-employment opportunities for women.

Environment

Tree plantation, clean village campaigns and environmental awareness programmes.
