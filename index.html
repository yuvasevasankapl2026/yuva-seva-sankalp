About Our Foundation

Yuva Seva Sankalp Foundation is a non-profit organisation working for education, health, social welfare, environment, women empowerment and youth development.

Education

Free coaching, scholarships and digital literacy.

Healthcare

Medical camps, blood donation and awareness programmes.

Social Welfare

Helping poor families and supporting community development.

Mission & Vision

🎯 Our Mission

To empower every citizen through education, healthcare, skill development and social service.

👁 Our Vision

To build a strong, educated and self-reliant India where everyone gets equal opportunities.

❤️ Our Values

Honesty, Transparency, Equality, Service and Community Participation.

Our Objectives

📚 Education

Provide scholarships, free coaching and digital education.

🏥 Healthcare

Organise health camps and blood donation drives.

🌳 Environment

Promote tree plantation and environmental awareness.

👩 Women Empowerment

Support self-help groups and skill development programmes.

👨 Youth Development

Career guidance, sports and leadership programmes.

🤝 Social Welfare

Help poor families, senior citizens and persons with disabilities.
